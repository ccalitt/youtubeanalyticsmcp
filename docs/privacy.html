Privacy Policy — Local Analytics

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, Local Analytics requests read-only access to:

  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): reports for your own channel, such as views, watch time, retention and traffic sources.
  • YouTube account data (youtube.readonly): your channel's name, statistics and video details.

The app cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything.

How data is used and stored

  • The app runs only on your own computer. There is no server, database or account operated by the developer.
  • Your sign-in token is stored in a file on your computer that only your user account can read.
  • Data fetched from YouTube is passed only to the AI assistant you run locally (Claude), to answer your own questions.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by this app.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the local token file.
